Open **Edit photo**, choose images, or paste a screenshot. Images stay on your
device. Tools can convert HEIC and HEIF when the browser cannot open them directly.

## Edit and compare

1. Choose a photo from **Photo** when you added several.
2. Choose **Crop**, **Adjust**, **Looks**, **Annotate**, or **Redact**.
3. Use **Undo** or **Redo** for any edit. One slider drag is one step.
4. Hold **Show original** to compare. **Fit** restores the preview position.

Drag crop corners to resize. Arrow keys move the focused crop;
Shift + arrow resizes it. **Straighten** keeps the corners filled.
For text, choose **Text**, tap the photo, and type directly on it.
Redactions are opaque black rectangles above other edits.

## Download the result

Saving removes location and other metadata. The original file stays unchanged.
The editor shows the output size when it must reduce a large photo; the default
maximum is 16.7 MP. A browser size refusal keeps your edits and offers an explicit
retry at a smaller size.

Choose the format, quality, and longest edge under **Export**. Only formats the
browser can encode are offered. **Download** saves the active photo;
**Download all as ZIP** creates one archive. Added photos keep their own edits.
If saving fails, the error stays beside the save action with **Try again**.

**Cancel** removes the photo from this session and asks before losing unsaved
edits. Closing, reloading, or navigating away also warns about unsaved edits.
