Open **Edit photo** and choose **Add photos**, drop images, or paste a screenshot. Images stay on your
device. Tools can convert HEIC and HEIF when the browser cannot open them directly.

## Edit and compare

1. Choose a photo in the image strip at the bottom when you added several. A dot marks unsaved edits. **+** adds more photos.
2. Choose **Crop**, **Adjust**, or **Mark up** on the right.
3. Use **Undo** or **Redo** for any edit. One slider drag is one step.
4. Choose **Compare** to see before and after side by side. Hold the O key to see the photo without adjustments for a moment.

Drag the photo on the grey canvas to move it, also under the bars. The mouse wheel, a pinch, and **+** or **−** zoom;
**Fit** shows the whole photo again. The ? key lists every shortcut.

Drag crop corners to resize. Arrow keys move the focused crop;
Shift + arrow resizes it. **Straighten** keeps the corners filled. **Auto** under **Adjust** calculates
brightness, contrast, saturation, and warmth from the photo; every slider stays free to change afterwards.

Under **Mark up**, choose pen, marker, arrow, rectangle, ellipse, text, or **Redact**. For text, choose a
point on the photo and type directly on it. Every mark appears under **Layers**: select it to move it,
resize it, or change its colour. Drag a layer or press Alt + arrow keys to change the order. Hidden marks
are not saved. Redactions are opaque black rectangles above other edits; they cannot be hidden.

## Download the result

Saving removes location and other metadata. The original file stays unchanged.
The editor shows the output size when it must reduce a large photo; the default
maximum is 16.7 MP. A browser size refusal keeps your edits and offers an explicit
retry at a smaller size.

Choose the format, quality, and longest edge under **Output**. Only formats the
browser can encode are offered. **Download** saves the active photo;
**Download all as ZIP** in the menu beside it creates one archive. Added photos keep their own edits.
If saving fails, the error stays above the save action with **Try again**.
If a ZIP fails because a photo is too large, accept its smaller size first, then
choose **Download all as ZIP** again. Photos count as saved after the whole archive succeeds.

**Close** removes the photo from this session and asks before losing unsaved
edits. Closing, reloading, or navigating away also warns about unsaved edits.
