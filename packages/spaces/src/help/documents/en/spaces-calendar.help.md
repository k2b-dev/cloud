**Calendar** in the Spaces sidebar, right below **My work**, shows what is planned across Cloud: the events and deadlines of every Space you can open, and the calendars that other apps offer you. Each calendar keeps its own color.

## Choose the view {icon="calendar-week"}

Choose **Day**, **Week**, **Month**, or **List** in the tabs in the page header. **List** shows the next 14 days, day by day. The arrows and **Today** move through the days. The view, the date, and the calendars you chose stay in the address, so a reload or a shared link opens the same view.

## Choose calendars {icon="calendar"}

:::reference
- **Calendars:** The **Calendars** chip opens the list of calendars, grouped by app. Tick a calendar to show it, or untick it to hide it.
- **All or none:** **All** and **None** next to an app choose all of its calendars at once.
- **Only one:** **Only** at the end of a row shows that calendar alone.
- **Default:** At first you see the calendars their apps suggest for you, for example every Space you are a member of. When you changed the choice, the chip is tinted, and **Reset** brings the default back.
- **Busy only:** Some calendars show you only when time is taken, not what it is. They say **free/busy only**, and their entries say **Busy**.
:::

## Open an event {icon="click"}

Select an event or a deadline to see when it is, how often it repeats, where it takes place, and which app and calendar it comes from. **Open in** takes you to the event in its own app, for example the item in its Space. You create and change events in their own app; this calendar only shows them.

## When a calendar does not answer {icon="alert-triangle"}

:::reference
- **Loading:** While some calendars are still loading, the line next to the chips says how many. What is already there stays in place.
- **No answer:** When an app does not answer, the line names it and offers **Try again**. The other calendars stay up to date, and the chooser marks the calendars of that app.
- **Nothing at all:** When Cloud cannot ask the apps for their calendars, the page says so and offers **Try again**. Your events are not gone.
:::
