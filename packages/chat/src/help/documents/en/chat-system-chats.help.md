A Space or Notebook chat brings its messages together. Each item has its own thread. Everyone with access to the Space or Notebook can read its chat.

## Find the chat {icon="messages"}

:::steps
1. Open **Chat** and expand **Spaces and Notebooks** at the end of the chat list.
2. Choose a Space or Notebook. **All threads** opens with messages from its items and the Space or Notebook itself.
3. Choose a thread to read just that item's messages.
:::

Only Spaces and Notebooks with a chat appear here. An empty section explains when a chat appears. If the section cannot load, choose **Try again** beside the error.

The short list shows recent threads, unread threads, and the open thread within one page. Choose **All N threads** or **Browse all threads** to browse more. Use **Search threads** and the **Open**, **Done**, or **Unread** filters. Choose **Show more threads** for the next page.

On large screens, a separate thread column provides the same search and filters. On a phone, the back arrow in a thread opens the thread overview.

## Write about an item {icon="message-plus"}

You need **Edit** or **Manage** access to the Space or Notebook.

:::steps
1. Choose **Talk about an item** below the threads, in the thread picker, or on the overview.
2. Find and select the task, event, or note in the element picker. Choose **Add**.
3. Write your message in the item's thread and send it.
:::

The thread's title and status come from the item. When available, its due date, assigned people, or event time appear beside it. Missing details stay hidden. Choose **Go to task ↗**, **Go to event ↗**, or **Go to note ↗** to open it. If opening the thread fails, the error stays visible and you can try again.

A message written in **All threads** goes to the Space or Notebook itself. A reply goes to the thread of the message you answer. The label under each message shows where it belongs.

## Manage access {icon="users"}

Change access in the Space or Notebook. Chat does not offer member editing, leaving, or manual thread names for these chats.

An address you cannot access shows **Thread unavailable**, without the item's title. Choose **Back to chats** to return to the list.
