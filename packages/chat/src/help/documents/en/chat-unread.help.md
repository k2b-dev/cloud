Unread messages are other people's messages after where you stopped reading. Your own messages and chat notices never count. Counts above 99 show **99+**. Your read position stays in sync across your devices.

## Continue reading {icon="messages"}

Chat marks messages read while you see them in a visible, focused window. Jumping to a linked message does not mark the messages in between read. If unread messages come before the linked message, **New messages** does not appear there.

:::reference
- A chat opens around your first unread message. **New messages** marks where you left off and stays until you leave the chat.
- The button that jumps back to **New messages** returns you to that position after you scroll elsewhere.
- A chat you can read through a group shows unread messages only after you open it once.
:::

## Mark a chat unread {icon="mail"}

:::steps
1. Open the chat's menu in the list.
2. Choose **Mark unread**.
:::

## Mark unread from a message {icon="mail"}

Use this when you have read something but want to come back to it later.

:::steps
1. Point at the message and select **…** beside it, or right-click the message. On a phone, touch and hold the message.
2. Choose **Mark unread from here**.
:::

**New messages** now starts at that message, the chat shows as unread in the list, and the next time you open it you land there. A message confirms the change; select **Undo** to put your read position back. If the change fails, nothing changes and you can select **Try again**.

:::reference
- Shortcut: hold **Alt** (**Option** on a Mac) while you choose the message.
- Your own messages have no **Mark unread from here**, because they never count as unread.
- Others still see what you have read.
- **Copy text** in the same menu copies the message's text.
:::

## Mark chats read {icon="checks"}

:::steps
1. Open the **Chats** section menu.
2. Choose **Mark all read**.
3. If 10 or more chats need marking, confirm the request.
:::

Press **Esc** in an open chat to mark that chat read.

If messages arrive while you are away from the tab, **New messages** takes you back to the unread messages you skipped. It loads the missing history if needed. Skipped messages stay unread until you see them.

## See what is for you {icon="at"}

**For you** at the top of the list shows messages that mention you and replies to your messages, from every chat, also from Spaces and Notebooks. The newest come first, and the number shows how many you have not read yet.

:::steps
1. Choose **For you** in the list.
2. Choose an entry to open its thread at the message.
:::

Reading the message in its thread marks it read here too. You only see messages you may read.

## Filter the list {icon="filter"}

Choose **Unread** above the list to show chats with new messages or your unread mark. A chat stays in that view after you read it, until you leave the filter. **All** returns to the whole list. The address keeps your filter when you reload or go back.
