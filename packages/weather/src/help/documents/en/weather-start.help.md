Weather tracks saved locations and shows current conditions, hourly forecasts, daily forecasts, and rain radar.

## Overview {icon="layout-grid"}

:::reference
- **Saved location:** A city saved to your account with its name, state, latitude, and longitude.
- **Forecast:** A location page shows current weather, hourly values, a daily forecast, and rain radar.
- **Display link:** Display opens a public monitor view that refreshes automatically.
- **Admin settings:** Administrators control fallback widget coordinates, cache duration, and the geocoding endpoint.
:::

## Use Weather {icon="route"}

:::reference
- **Add a location:** Search for a German city and select a result. Weather saves its coordinates to your account.
- **Start from an empty overview:** If no location is saved, use Add location without selecting a forecast first.
- **Read a forecast:** Open a saved location for current conditions, hourly and daily forecasts, measurements, and rain radar.
- **Open a display:** Use Display to choose the size, a light or dark theme, and a simple or detailed view.
- **Remove a location:** Removing a location deletes it from your account and returns you to the Weather overview.
:::

:::info Data coverage
The current location search is limited to German cities. Forecast data may be unavailable when the configured provider has no data for the selected coordinates.
:::
