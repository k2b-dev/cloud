Dashboard is your personal start page and overview across Cloud. It shows widgets from the apps you use on one board, with shortcuts you choose above it.

## Overview {icon="layout-grid"}

:::reference
- **Board:** Widgets stand in one order, the same on every device. Each widget is small, medium, or large; on a phone, small widgets stand side by side and the others take the full width.
- **Default board:** Until you change something, you see the widgets your apps suggest for you. New suggestions appear on it by themselves.
- **Shortcuts:** Shortcuts point to a Cloud app or to a relative, HTTP(S), or mailto link.
- **Saved to your account:** Your board and shortcuts apply on every device after you sign in.
:::

## Arrange your board {icon="settings"}

:::reference
- **Edit:** Choose **Edit**, or touch and hold a widget, to arrange the board. Choose **Done** to save it, or **Cancel** to keep the board as it was.
- **Move:** Drag a widget to its new place. On a touch screen, hold it briefly first. With a keyboard, focus a widget and use the arrow keys.
- **Size:** Choose **Small**, **Medium**, or **Large** at the bottom of a widget. A widget offers only the sizes its app supports.
- **Remove:** Choose × on a widget, or focus it and press **Delete**.
- **Add:** Choose **Widget** or **Add widget** to open the gallery. It shows the widgets you may use, grouped by app, with a preview of your own data in the size you choose. **Suggested for you** lists suggestions that are not on your board yet.
- **Default:** Returns to the default board. After **Done**, new suggestions appear on your board again by themselves.
- **Shortcuts:** While editing, choose × on a shortcut to remove it, or **Shortcut** to add one.
:::

:::info Settings scope
Dashboard settings require a session of a person, not of a service account. They are stored with your account and apply on every device where you sign in.
:::
