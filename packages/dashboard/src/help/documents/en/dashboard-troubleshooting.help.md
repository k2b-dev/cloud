Dashboard combines content published by several apps. One unavailable widget should not prevent the rest of the page from loading.

## Common symptoms {icon="lifebuoy"}

:::reference
- **A widget is missing:** Choose **Edit**, then **Add widget**, and search for it in the gallery. The gallery lists only widgets you may use; if it is not there, ask an administrator whether its app is installed and whether you have access.
- **A widget says it is not responding:** Choose **Try again** in that widget. If it fails again, open the owning app to confirm that the app is running and you can access its data.
- **A widget loads slowly:** Each widget loads on its own, so the others appear as soon as their apps answer. After a few seconds the widget says that its app is taking longer than usual; if the app does not answer in time, the widget shows **Try again**.
- **A widget says "No access any more":** Your access to that app or its data changed. Remove the widget in the edit mode, or ask for access again.
- **A widget says "Nothing to show right now":** Its app has no content for you at the moment. The widget keeps its place and fills again when there is something to show.
- **A new widget does not appear by itself:** Once you arranged your own board, new widgets wait under **Suggested for you** in the gallery. **Default** returns you to the default board, which takes suggestions in by itself.
- **A shortcut is gone:** App shortcuts can disappear when access to the app changes. Add it again while editing.
- **A custom link does not open:** Confirm that it is a supported relative, HTTP(S), or mailto link and that the browser allows the target.
- **Changes appear on one device only:** Reload the page on the other device after you chose **Done**. Signed-in settings are stored with the user account.
:::

## Keep the start page useful {icon="square-plus"}

- Keep widgets you check regularly; remove status widgets that no longer affect daily work.
- Give the widget you look at first the large size, and use small widgets for single numbers.
- Prefer a small number of recognizable shortcuts over duplicating the complete app rail.
- Open the source app for editing or detailed diagnosis. Dashboard widgets are summaries and entry points.
