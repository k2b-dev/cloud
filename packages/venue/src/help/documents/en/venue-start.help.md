Venues manages staffed places that need public opening status, shift signup, public page content, and visitor feedback. The Venues overview lists every venue you can access and offers templates or a blank venue before you select one.

## Overview {icon="layout-grid"}

:::reference
- **Venue:** One staffed place such as a cafe, office hours desk, service counter, or recurring event location.
- **Opening hours:** Regular weekly hours plus exceptions for single dates decide whether the public page says the venue is open. An exception closes the venue for the day or opens it as a special opening with its own times.
- **Shift:** A staffing slot with target people, optional maximum people, and assigned users.
- **Public section:** An editable block on the public page. Sections can be markdown, menu, notice, or links.
:::

## First useful path {icon="route"}

:::reference
- **Create or use a template:** Before you have a venue, the overview offers the starter templates and **Start blank** directly; later, **New venue** offers the same. You only enter a name. The slug follows the name, and you can change it under **Advanced**. When another venue already uses the slug, the field says so and keeps everything you entered.
- **Follow the setup steps:** Until a venue has shifts, its schedule shows admins **Set up this venue**: set opening hours or shifts, add shifts when staffing opens the venue, and give your team access. Each step opens the right settings tab. The list goes away once the venue has shifts or every step is done. Everyone else reads **No shifts are planned here yet.**
- **Configure the schedule:** In the settings, **General** holds the public page switch, how staff sign up, the time zone, and the public opening logic. **Schedule** holds the weekly opening hours, exceptions, and recurring shifts. A new shift can cover several weekdays at once and becomes one shift per weekday.
- **Publish what visitors need:** Open the public page from the workspace sidebar. Admins switch it off and on with **Public page on** in **General**; while it is off, its link shows only that the venue is not available, the same as for a venue that does not exist, with a link to **Open in Venues**. Sections with **Show on the public page** switched on and the feedback form appear there. Visitors choose 1 to 5 stars before they can send feedback; a comment is optional.
- **Staff the venue:** Staff users take visible shifts with **Take shift**. Admin users can also remove people from shifts.
:::
