### "To the newest message" hid behind "Waiting for you"

- **What happened:** In the fifth and sixth Preview tours, a chat scrolled up showed the pill "Zur neuesten Nachricht" half covered by the row "Wartet auf dich" above the field, and the conversation showed through between the row's chips. The first fix (#3453) never merged, so the sixth tour saw the same fault again.
- **Root cause:** The pill (`@k2b/ui` Chat.Timeline) floats 8 px above the timeline's end; the row lies on the same free end. Both had their own layer and neither knew the other. The row faded the text behind it with a colour gradient that stayed transparent across its upper half.
- **Error class:** Two floating elements over the same edge, each placed by its own owner, without one reservation for the stack; and a fade drawn as a colour instead of a mask.
- **Guard:** The Assistant's chat layout names the stack: two layers, the free end (`--assistant-free-end`), and a fixed place for the pill above the row's slot, whether a row shows or not. While the row shows, the timeline is masked out from the row down to the field and fades above it. The waiting row's browser test checks at 1440 and 390 that nothing covers the pill, that the pill stays above the row, that the band from the row to the field looks the same with the conversation hidden, and that the pill keeps its place without the row.
