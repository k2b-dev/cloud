### Pruned snapshot coverage must reach every shared-text reader

- **What happened:** Sending an API-updated reply failed before command creation, increased its content revision and left subsequent reads in conflict. Successful sends could also leave their shared document close pending.
- **Root cause:** Mail records `doc_idle_through` before pruning absent events from the shared stream, but its snapshot loader returned only `doc_cursor`. Freeze could publish its marker and then replay from that stale cursor. Snapshot recovery misclassified the covered prefix as lost history, incremented the revision and marked the draft incomplete. A skipped locked row could also be pruned past its coverage without saving its idle proof. Pruning also excluded sent tombstones whose document close was still pending. The production retention exception matches this chain; its minified close error name alone does not identify an exception class.
- **Error class:** Divergent snapshot coverage across readers and lifecycle boundaries.
- **Guard:** `draft-document-retention.integration.test.ts` creates a reply through the public service, replaces it, advances another draft and prunes before sending; it checks exact outbound content, unchanged revision and complete history. A separate sent-draft test leaves the thaw marker uncovered, advances the shared stream, then checks that pruning retains the close replay boundary and that close finishes without changing content or revision. An uncovered synchronous replay gap must leave revision/history unchanged, release its owned freeze and permit retry. Shared-text unit tests reject missing uncovered snapshot targets, preserve background recovery and check freeze cleanup. A locked-row guard pins pruning to the last committed proof; the pending-close branch preserves the existing partial indexes. A background recovery rereads coverage under the pruning lock before classifying loss. Normal send preparation adds no SQL statements; coverage is included in its existing load query.
