### The QR scanner waited on a camera picture that froze after its first frame

- **What happened:** WebKit runs of `packages/ui/src/inputs/qr-scanner.browser.test.ts` timed out now and then in different cases: the turning camera never showed its portrait picture (#3364, #3421), and the detector cases never read or failed (#3452, #3713). Each report showed a playing preview with its first picture and no later progress.
- **Root cause:** WebKit's player sometimes shows only the first picture of a camera stream although `play()` resolved. In Playwright's WebKit, 8 of 400 canvas streams froze this way. Static and changing pictures, other capture modes, and a canvas in the document all froze at a similar rate. In all 8 cases, attaching the same stream again restored the full frame rate. `QrScanner` treated any resolved `play()` with a picture size as a working camera. qr-scanner reads only new pictures, so the scanner showed its instructions over a still picture and never scanned or reported a problem.
- **Error class:** A media consumer treats the first picture of a live stream as proof that the stream runs.
- **Guard:** `QrScanner` counts a picture as started only after the player shows two more pictures. A picture that stands still after `play()` gets the stream attached again once by itself, before "Try again" appears. The browser test "starts a camera picture again by itself when the player freezes on its first picture" holds frame callbacks until the stream is attached again. Without the fix, the test times out with the same report as CI.
