### Rows arriving during a press moved the pressed command away from the pointer

- **What happened:** `catalog and resource recovery in real panel at 1440px` failed again in WebKit on #2218 (#2079, #2140). The press trace from #2178 showed `pointerdown` on the Notes command row, then `pointerup` and `click` on the composer section 4 ms later, while the command list stayed open. The row had moved away from under the pointer.
- **Root cause:** The composer's command list grows upward from the field. Rows that arrive while the person presses a row, such as the Assistant's searched sections for `/notes`, change the list's height and move the visible rows. The release then lands beside the pressed row, so the browser sends the click to a common ancestor and no command runs. #2154 and #2176 covered a replaced row and a rebuilt menu, but not a row that stays and moves. For people this is a layout shift during a press, and the click does nothing.
- **Error class:** A list moves its rows while a pointer is down on one of them.
- **Guard:** While a pointer is down on a command row, the composer keeps the list it showed at the press and applies new rows after the release (also after a cancelled pointer or a blur of the window). `chat-composer.browser.test.ts` lets server rows arrive between `mouse.down()` and `mouse.up()` with `pressAcrossRebuild()`, and checks that the row has not moved and that the command runs. Without the change, the row moved and the test failed. A second test checks that the held rows show once the pointer is released elsewhere.
