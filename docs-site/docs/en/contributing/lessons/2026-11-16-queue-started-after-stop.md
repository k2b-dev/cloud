### A stopped chat started its next queued message by itself

- **What happened:** A person stopped Gustav; eight seconds later the queue started the next message, which then waited for an approval, so the chat could not be archived (#108).
- **Root cause:** The queue drain advanced after any end of the current turn, including a person's Stop; the selection could become stale before promotion under the chat lock; archiving refused chats with queued entries, and its conflict named only a running response. The archive decision also needed a fresh snapshot after taking the chat lock when a promotion was in flight.
- **Error class:** A person's explicit Stop treated like a normal end of work by automatic follow-up logic.
- **Guard:** `queue-hold.integration.test.ts` checks stale selection against the real promotion transaction, direct admission for people and service accounts, foreground versus background Stop, a late Stop, Stop after Send now, the global sweep and restore. It checks an aged hold timestamp without claiming to advance runtime timers. `controller.behavior.test.ts` checks that only `queue_waiting` falls back to the queue and preserves full content; `turn.test.ts` checks the CLI recovery hint. `routes.integration.test.ts` and `archive-blocker.test.ts` cover the archive conflict, including a promotion race and the atomic discard count. `confirm-archive.test.ts` covers the archive warning in both languages with zero, one, multiple or unknown waiting entries; `chat.test.ts` covers the CLI receipt and structured output.
