### Rows in workspace columns touched the left edge

- **What happened:** After every column boundary got its shadow, rows, selections, and group titles in levels sat flush against the column's left edge, while a gap stayed on the right. In Core › Administration › AI the selected provider and model rows began at the shadow and ended 12 to 15 px before the next one.
- **Root cause:** A level body had no inline padding. Its only side space was the stable scrollbar gutter at the end, so the right side was as wide as the scrollbar and the left side was zero. A navigation pane padded both sides but added the gutter at the end as well. Without the shadow, the gap between columns hid this. Applications compensated with their own padding: `AdminAreaPane` with `px-[var(--ui-space-shell)]`, AI details with `px-4`, and the levels demo with a CSS override.
- **Error class:** A column inset that came from a side effect (the scrollbar gutter) instead of from the primitive, so it differed between sides and platforms.
- **Guard:** `packages/ui/src/layout/workspace-column-shadow.browser.test.ts` measures every row and the group title in every level and navigation pane, at 1440 and 390 px. Each row's fill must start and end 0.5 rem from the column's side edges, the group title must line up with the level head, and hover and focus must not move a row. The test fails against the stylesheet before this fix. The test also grows each column until it scrolls and requires the same insets. The layout keeps both insets in `packages/ui`, with the end inset as a fixed 0.5 rem scroll track that is always present: WebKit reserves no `scrollbar-gutter` without overflow, which the first version missed and the WebKit CI run caught (end inset 0).
