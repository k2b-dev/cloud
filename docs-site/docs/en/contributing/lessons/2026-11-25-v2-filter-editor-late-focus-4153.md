### A late focus move in the filter editor took a field away while it was being typed in

- **What happened:** In the merge queue the test "a number the field cannot read keeps the active filter" (#4153) failed: after `fill("")` the price editor and its field were gone, and typing waited for a field that no longer existed.
- **Root cause:** The editor moved the focus to the first control of its values in a timer after it mounted. On a loaded machine the timer ran after the person (here the test) had already put the focus into the "Von" field and emptied it. The field lost the focus, applied its empty value on `change` as "no range", the filter went away, and with its statement the editor closed. Clearing every bound also closed the editor on its own.
- **Error class:** A deferred focus move that does not check whether the person has moved the focus since, so it can take the focus from a field in use; and an editor whose last value removes the thing it is attached to.
- **Guard:** The editor sets its first focus once, through the popover's `initialFocus`, with no later move. Emptying every bound in an open editor keeps it open with the filter as a dashed draft. `packages/ui/src/v2/filter-bar.browser.test.ts` checks that the focus goes straight into the values and stays in a field the person focused, and that an emptied range keeps its editor; the #4153 test passed 20 times in a row under 4× CPU throttling in Chromium and in WebKit.
