### A jump to a place far down scrolled the whole v2 workspace

- **What happened:** A v2 page opened at a section far down (`/me?groups=all#groups`) moved its whole workspace up. The sidebar column stood empty, the head was gone, and empty room stood at the bottom. `scrollIntoView` did the same.
- **Root cause:** The frame of a workspace (`.k2-ws`, its side and main column, `.k2-pane`, `.k2-split`, the drawer) clipped its content with `overflow: hidden`. Such a box hides its overflow from the person, but the browser can still scroll it. When the pane could not bring the place to the top by itself, the fragment jump and `scrollIntoView` scrolled the frame around it as well. In the test page the workspace moved by 654 px.
- **Error class:** A box that must never move clips with `overflow: hidden`, which a script, a focus or a fragment jump can still scroll.
- **Guard:** The frame clips with `overflow: clip`, which nothing can scroll. `packages/ui/src/v2/fragment-jump.browser.test.ts` opens a page at `#deep` at 1024 and 1440 px and brings the place into view by a script, in Chromium and WebKit. Only the pane's scroll may move; the sidebar and the head stay where they stood before the jump.
- **Found on the way:** On the phone a split's panes stand one under the other, and the page's scroll was the split's `overflow: hidden`, which no finger and no wheel moves. Only a script could scroll it, as the floating-action test did, so the end of such a page was out of reach. Now the split of the main column scrolls itself on the phone, without a bar of its own. `fab-room.browser.test.ts` scrolls with the wheel until the last row stops moving, as a person does, in Chromium and WebKit.
