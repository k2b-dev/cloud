### v2 lists cut numbers and squeezed text, and every check stayed green

- **What happened:** With realistic worst-case data (break-ui, 25.11.) the v2 table cut "12.345.678,90 €" to "12.345.678,…", the key figures of a record cut an amount to "−12.345.678,9", a row with a long deed squeezed its text to one letter per line at 1024 px, a table ran out of the window at 200 % zoom, and a person's name in a table cell was cut through a letter without "…". The recipe test measured 14 of 14 recipes green.
- **Root cause:** Every cut followed one rule ("long text is cut with …") that also hit numbers; the end of a row took all the room its deed asked for; a table's minimum width reached the page through its scroller; and "…" only reaches text that runs inline in the box that cuts it, not text in a flex item. The test measured only 1440 and 390 px, and `measureV2Layout` had no notion of a number, of text under a control, or of a cut through a sign.
- **Error class:** A layout rule for text applied to values whose meaning changes when cut, and a guard that measures only the widths where the layout happens to fit.
- **Guard:** `Column numeric` (implied by `align: "end"`) never cuts and measures its column; a row with a long deed (over 24 signs) puts it under the text in a column under 640 px, never cutting it; the table's scroller is a size container that drops `drop` columns by its own room. `measureV2Layout` reports text on a control that is not its own, cut numbers and cuts without "…" (`testing/measure-v2-text.ts`), and `skill-recipe-states.browser.test.ts` measures every recipe state, worst case included, at 1440, 1024, 390 and 320 px and at 200 % zoom, and fails on any part beyond the window.
