### Chat acted on a step before its transition had ended

- **What happened:** Two Chat browser tests failed on unrelated pull requests. In WebKit, a press on Info right after closing chat info never opened it again ([#2487](https://github.com/k2b-dev/cloud/issues/2487)). In Chromium, "Back to chats" during send preparation left the panel at `/app/chat/panel` instead of the chat ([#2503](https://github.com/k2b-dev/cloud/issues/2503)).
- **Root cause:** On a wide screen, a closing detail panel gives the chat its room back 140 ms after it starts to slide out, in one step. The test pressed Info during the 140 ms, and the step moved Info by 424 px between the press and the release, so the click went to the header. For a chat opened from the list, "Back to chats" first went back in history and only the `popstate` handler refused to leave. The address showed the list until the handler put the chat back. The test read the address after a notice that the earlier Archive refusal had already shown.
- **Error class:** Acting on or checking an earlier signal before a transition ends: refusing a navigation after history has moved, or pressing a control that a delayed layout step still moves.
- **Guard:** "Back to chats" and the way up check the pending send before they go back in history. `ChatPanel.browser.test.ts` opens the chat from the list after the island has mounted, checks for the list-origin history entry, and requires that no `popstate` happened. The [testing guide](/en/docs/contributing/testing#wait-for-conditions-not-time) asks for `settled(page)` after closing a detail panel before the next press. The Info reopening test in `ChatWorkspace.browser.test.ts` follows it.
