### A v2 workspace head cut its title down to a few letters beside its actions

- **What happened:** The head of a Space on `@k2b/ui/v2` (W3: its name, four views, a search, its people, a "…" menu and the main action) gave the Space's name 160 of its 583 px at 1024 px beside the sidebar. Even a name of four letters ("Büro") was cut to 18 px.
- **Root cause:** Actions beside tabs keep their full room (`flex: none`), so the search kept its 184 px field and every action kept its words. Only the title and the tabs could give way, and the title, a flex item with `min-width: 0`, shrank as far as the actions asked. Below 768 px the phone rules fold the head's actions, but a tablet window beside its sidebar is wider than a phone and its head is narrower than a desktop's.
- **Error class:** A give-way order that exists only for the window's width (the phone) and not for the width of the place itself, so a narrow place in a wide window keeps the wide layout and lets the wrong part give way.
- **Guard:** The workspace head is a container (`k2-head`). Below 800 px of its own width, its search keeps only its symbol, and the actions without `keep` leave for the head's "…" menu, while the main action stays. The search's Escape fold follows the stylesheet instead of the window's width. `packages/ui/src/v2/head-title.browser.test.ts` measures a Space's head at 390, 1024 and 1440 px in Chromium and WebKit, with a long name and a short one. It checks the title's width, the actions that show, that nothing leaves the row, the open search over the head and its Escape fold.
