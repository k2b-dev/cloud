### A v2 workspace head cut its title down to a few letters beside its actions

- **What happened:** The head of a Space on `@k2b/ui/v2` (W3: its name, four views, a search, its people, a "…" menu and the main action) gave the Space's name 36 of its 583 px at 1024 px in Cloud's shell, and 239 px at 1280 px. The views kept "Liste", "Tabelle" and "Mehr", and the people kept their place.
- **Root cause:** The bar's fold (`bar-fold.ts`) moves only buttons into the "…", so the people, which are no button, never gave way. Once nothing could fold, the start shortened: the title took the shortage first (its weight 1000) and the tabs only after it.
- **Error class:** A give-way order that follows how things are built (which parts are buttons, which part has the larger weight), not what the head is for: the head names its page.
- **Guard:** People in the end (an `Avatar` that is no button) give way with the buttons; the "…" lists nothing for them, as they have no deed. In a workspace's head the tabs give way before the title (a shrink weight far above the title's), whole tab by whole tab into "Mehr". `packages/ui/src/v2/head-title.browser.test.ts` measures a Space's head in Cloud's shell at 1024, 1280 and 1366 px, where the name keeps at least 340 px, and at 1440 px, in Chromium and WebKit. It also covers a short name, the "…" listing what gave way, a head without a "…" of its own, and a wide head where nothing gives way.
