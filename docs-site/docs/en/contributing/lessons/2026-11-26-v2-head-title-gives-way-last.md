### A v2 workspace head cut its title down to a few letters beside its actions

- **What happened:** The head of a Space on `@k2b/ui/v2` (W3: its name, four views, a search, its people, a "…" menu and the main action) gave the Space's name 171 of its 583 px at 1024 px in Cloud's shell, and 427 px at 1280 px. A four-letter name ("Büro") was cut to 18 px. On the phone, a head's buttons without `keep` disappeared, even where nothing took them in (Bearbeiten in a recipe's detail; Löschen and Neu erzeugen in OAuth).
- **Root cause:** Actions beside tabs kept their full room, and the title and the tabs shrank in proportion to their widths. The give-way order existed only for the window's width (the phone). It hid what had no `keep`, whether or not a "…" listed it.
- **Error class:** A give-way rule keyed to the window instead of the place. A fold that removes deeds by convention instead of moving them where a person still finds them.
- **Guard:** A head with a title folds below 1100 px of its own width: the search to its symbol while it holds no words, and the buttons without `keep` into the head's "…". The "…" (an icon-only `Menu`) lists what gave way, with its names and deeds. A head without a "…" keeps its actions. The tabs shrink before the title. The bar marks its title and its "…" itself (`data-title`, `data-more`), so the rules need no `:has()` that a change deep in the page makes the browser check again (`shell-restyle`, `css-has-scope`). `packages/ui/src/v2/head-title.browser.test.ts` measures a Space's head in Cloud's shell at 1024, 1280, 1366 and 1440 px and on the phone, in Chromium and WebKit. It also covers a short name, the open search with and without words, the "…" listing what gave way, and a head without a "…".
