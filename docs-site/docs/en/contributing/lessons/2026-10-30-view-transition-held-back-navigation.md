### A view transition held back the navigation it was meant to animate

- **What happened:** `Chat info reopening > info stays reachable after X and preserves Back on desktop` in `packages/chat/src/frontend/ChatWorkspace.browser.test.ts` failed in WebKit, also in a merge group on `main`: after the click on the Info toggle, `waitForURL("**?panel=info")` timed out after 5 s (#2116).
- **Root cause:** An enhanced link moves history only inside the update of the view transition that the click starts, and the browser runs that update only after it has captured the old state. On a busy WebKit, that transition's lifecycle took seconds. With one CPU, 9 of 10 runs of the test failed. The close button starts no transition, so the click itself did not fall into a running one.
- **Error class:** An animation blocks a navigation: work done only for the animation holds back the change the person asked for.
- **Guard:** `startViewTransition()` in `packages/ssr/src/nav-history.ts` skips a transition that has not reached its update within `VIEW_TRANSITION_UPDATE_BUDGET_MS` (200 ms); the update still runs, only without the animation. `packages/ssr/test/unit/nav.test.ts` simulates a slow capture and checks that the update runs and the transition is skipped once, and that a timely transition is not skipped. With one CPU, the test then passed 10 of 10 runs, with the address changed 24–574 ms after the click.
