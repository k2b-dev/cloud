### A Chat link click changed no address, and the timeout said nothing about the page

- **What happened:** After #2053, `Chat info reopening > info stays reachable after X and preserves Back on desktop` in `packages/chat/src/frontend/ChatWorkspace.browser.test.ts` still failed twice in WebKit: the click on the Info toggle succeeded, but `waitForURL("**?panel=info")` timed out after 5 s (#2116).
- **Root cause:** Not yet known. An enhanced link moves history only inside the update of the view transition that the click starts; a local trace showed the click, the transition start, its update with `openInfo` about 0.4 s later, and then `pushState`. A stalled capture, a lost click, or an early return in `openInfo` all end the same way. The `waitForURL` timeout carried no page state, so the failures could not tell them apart. It was not reproduced in 25 local runs.
- **Error class:** A failure report shows only one side of a state change, so a lost change cannot be placed.
- **Guard:** `waitForURL` timeouts from `launchBrowser()` pages now carry the navigation diagnosis, and its main-thread probe reports whether a view transition is still active. A wait does not count as a navigation of the test, so `pageErrors()` keeps its contract. `packages/ui/test/browser.browser.test.ts` checks the diagnosis with a transition whose update never ends. #2116 stays open until the next occurrence names the cause.
