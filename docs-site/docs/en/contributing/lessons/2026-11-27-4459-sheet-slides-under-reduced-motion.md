### A v2 sheet slid in although the person asked for less motion

- **What happened:** On a phone, a v2 menu, popover or dialog opens as a sheet from the bottom. With `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` on a touch screen, the sheet still slid up over about a second (W7 measured its top from 826 to 544 px in Chromium). With a mouse it appeared in place.
- **Root cause:** `@ui-sdk/core` 0.1.0-beta.14 moves a touch screen's sheet its scroll-snap way. That way plays its slide in and out and its spring after a swipe as `translate` animations, and it does not check reduced motion. Core's pointer way, used without touch, fades instead. `@k2b/ui` had no test of the sheet with reduced motion, so the gap went unnoticed.
- **Error class:** Motion that a dependency plays, not v2.css, and that no test watched under reduced motion.
- **Guard:** Under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`, v2.css sets `translate: none !important` on a sheet with `data-driver="scroll-snap"`. An important declaration outranks an animation in the cascade, so the sheet stands at its place from its first frame; at rest the host's scroll carries it, never a translate. `packages/ui/src/v2/menus.browser.test.ts` and `overlays.browser.test.ts` record the sheet's top on every frame after it opens, for the menu, the popover and the dialog, with a mouse and on a touch screen, and expect one top only. `packages/ui/src/v2/v2-css.test.ts` checks that every animation and transition in v2.css has a rule under reduced motion that stops it.
- **Known limits:** The CSS guard reads v2.css only; motion that core plays elsewhere shows only in the browser tests. The gap in core's scroll-snap way remains until core handles reduced motion itself.
