### Continuous month title lagged in WebKit CI

- **What happened:** The WebKit case "arrows align the first's week, and rapid steps accumulate across three months" in `calendar-month.browser.test.ts` read "November 2026" after three quick Next steps, although the grid had reached the week of 2026-12-28 and the test expected "January 2027". It failed in run 38585934929 and in job 118200934376 of merge-queue run 39293693729 ([#1139](https://github.com/k2b-dev/cloud/issues/1139)). On a busy machine, the case failed in 5 of 200 local runs.
- **Root cause:** The test pinned the date with `page.clock.setFixedTime`, which also installs Playwright's fake clock. The month view reads its scroll position in an animation frame, and under the fake clock that frame is a timer that is not tied to the rendering. The test waited only for the scroll position and then read the title once, so the read could come before the frame. With the page's own frames, the title changed in the same rendering update in which the position arrived. The product was correct.
- **Error class:** Faked animation frames in a browser test.
- **Guard:** `test-clock` flags new `.clock.setFixedTime(` and `.clock.setSystemTime(` calls on a page or context; a test whose subject is fake timers says so with `// playwright clock: <reason>`. The month test pins only `Date` with `pinPageClock` and waits until the grid rests at the week and the header names the month, so a wrong title fails with the week and title it showed.
