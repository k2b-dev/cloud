### Completed authoring must not become a pause

- **What happened:** Gustav showed a pause after a version had been submitted and its examples had passed.
- **Root cause:** The provider wrapper checked the turn budget before the closing model response and always exposed a stop receipt, even when its own tool-result snapshot confirmed every draft was submitted.
- **Error class:** A terminal guard overwrites an already completed domain outcome.
- **Guard:** `code-authoring-turn.test.ts` simulates a build with testing, repair and submission; regression cases assert that completed submissions get exactly one tool-free closing answer naming unfinished or unstarted requests. Only that successful answer suppresses the pause. Later edits, unfinished drafts and an exhausted closing allowance retain the receipt. Executor regressions also verify the saved assistant message and completed turn after a tool call, provider error, thrown error or rejected cost admission in the closing attempt. Without the local terminal block, nessi closes an unfinished turn without saving the stop metadata. Cancellation remains an abort. Cost-admission tests cover disabled, unlimited and bypassed quotas and background turns without chat admission; these keep the token guard.
