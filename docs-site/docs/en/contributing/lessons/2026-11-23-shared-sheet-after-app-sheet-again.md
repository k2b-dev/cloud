### An app sheet after the shared one cancelled breakpoints on every app page

- **What happened:** The 0.85 screenshot comparison against 0.83 showed the phone header above the app at 768 and 1440 px in Contacts, FAQ, Files, Gustav, Notebooks and Weather. Weather's forecast and rain radar no longer stood side by side, and the Contacts list lost its e-mail and phone text. Tinted detail buttons replaced the outline ones in Contacts, Files and Grids.
- **Root cause:** #3928 linked an app's `app.css` after `global.css`. An app sheet compiles without Cloud's theme, so it emits theme-free utilities such as `flex` and `grid-cols-1` but no breakpoint variant such as `md:hidden` or `md:grid-cols-2`; those exist only in `global.css`. Both sheets put their utilities in `@layer utilities` at equal specificity, so the later app sheet's `flex` beat the shared `md:hidden`, in shared and in app markup alike. Unlayered app rules such as `.k2b-ui .k2b-button.contacts-details-main` also began to beat shared rules of equal specificity that had won before. `scoped-variants` did not report the header: it read a template's static parts as one class list and the strings in its substitutions on their own, so `flex ${authenticated ? "md:hidden" : ""}` never met. It also scans only shared UI, and its premise that an app sheet holds both sides of a pair is wrong for every app sheet without a theme. This pull request restores the 0.83 order; #3921, an app utility that loses to a shared one on the same property, is open again.
- **Error class:** A change to the cascade order between two stylesheets, verified against one direction of the conflict while the other direction covers every page.
- **Guard:** `define-app.css-order.browser.test.ts` renders the real app document with a shared sheet that has the theme and an app sheet in the shape every app ships, and requires `flex md:hidden` to be hidden and `grid-cols-1 md:grid-cols-2` to have two columns at 1440 px; it fails with the #3928 order. `scoped-variants` now joins a template's static parts with each string its substitutions can add, so the header's pair reports.
