### Two copies of @k2b/ui took each other's history markers

- **What happened:** In #4275, `Dialog.confirm` started to answer only after its history entry had left, so a caller that navigates on the answer replaces its own page entry. In WebKit, revoking an agent's API key left Back on the revoked key's address, because the answer had come before the entry was gone. A question that an island's own copy of the library asked, after the page's copy had asked one, then never answered.
- **Root cause:** Each copy of the library (every island bundles its own) numbered its history markers from 1. The page's copy took the island's marker 1 for its own, and its popstate listener stopped the island's synthetic Back with `stopImmediatePropagation`. The island's copy never heard its Back arrive. Separately, the v2 question gave its entry back only when its surface closed, after the answer.
- **Error class:** State keyed by a per-module counter that several bundled copies share through one global (`history.state`). It looks unique inside one copy and collides across copies.
- **Guard:** `internal/dialog-history.ts` starts each copy's markers at a random multiple of 2^20, and a question gives its entry back at its answer and answers once the history has settled. `packages/ui/src/v2/overlays.browser.test.ts` asks from the island's own copy after the page's copy has asked, and checks where the history stands when an answer arrives, in Chromium and WebKit.
- **Follow-up:** An answer that waits for the history must not wait for a Back that never comes. In WebKit a page set from markup takes no history entries, so the synthetic Back fired no popstate and the answer of `Dialog.confirm` never arrived (the recipe states test). A history of one entry that is still one after a push took no entry (it has nothing ahead that the push could cut): nothing is held, and no Back is sent or awaited. A longer history may keep its length while it does take an entry, so length alone is no sign there. When the page leaves while a Back is on its way, nothing waits any longer either. The dialogs of `dialog-core` wait through the same `settled()` and are bounded with it. `overlays.browser.test.ts` answers a question on such a page.
- **Known gap:** The copies order their own entries only. When copy A opened an entry, then copy B, then A again, a Back that lands on B's entry makes A close its lower dialog too, because A cannot tell where B's entry stands among its own.
