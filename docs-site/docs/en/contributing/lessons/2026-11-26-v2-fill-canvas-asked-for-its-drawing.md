### A drawing that was the whole pane hid the Assistant's composer

- **What happened:** The Assistant on `@k2b/ui/v2` draws its chat in a `Canvas height="fill"`, as a column with no height of its own: a history that scrolls inside, and a composer under it. With a long history the composer was out of sight. Alone in its column the composer was cut off at the bottom of the canvas. In a split on the phone the pane also grew past the screen. The app worked around both by placing its drawing absolutely.
- **Root cause:** Two things. A fill canvas was a block, so a child without a height took its content's height instead of the canvas's, and the canvas clipped it; that cut the composer off in every layout. And on the phone, a pane with a fill canvas in a split keeps at least its `min-content` height (so a long list beside it cannot squeeze the drawing below 240 px). A block canvas's `min-content` is everything its drawing holds, so the pane grew by the whole history.
- **Error class:** A box that should hand its height down hands down nothing (a block lets a child grow to its content), and a minimum taken from intrinsic content (`min-content`) counts everything a child holds, not only the minimum the box means to keep.
- **Guard:** A fill canvas is a grid of one `minmax(0, 1fr)` row, so its one child, the drawing, takes the whole height and scrolls inside. It also sets `contain: size`, so it asks its pane for its own 240 px only. `packages/ui/src/v2/head-media.browser.test.ts` ("a tall drawing that is the whole pane…") checks a chat alone in its column and in a split, at 390 and 1440 px, in Chromium and WebKit. In each case the pane ends on the screen, the composer is in sight, the history scrolls inside, and the page does not scroll. Without the grid, all four cases are red. Without `contain: size`, the split at 390 px is red.
