### A stalled CI runner failed a passing Mail file in the Sync cleanup

- **What happened:** In `integration (5)` of #3939, `view-count-cache.integration.test.ts` passed all 17 tests and then failed `(unnamed)`: the stream delete of the Mail preload's cleanup (`scripts/fixtures/test-sync.ts`) got no answer within its 5 s request limit (#3945).
- **Root cause:** The whole runner stood still for about 13 s. The Docker health checks of rsql and of Garage, every 2 s otherwise, have no entry from 00:54:11 to 00:54:25, and Bun's own error output paused for 7.6 s in the middle of one stack trace. None of the 42 integration jobs checked shows another pause over 4 s. The cleanup itself was not at fault: it opens a fresh connection, its stream list was answered, and the process Sync had drained before. The job recorded no memory, CPU or disk pressure and printed no NATS log, so the cause of the stall stays unknown.
- **Error class:** A failure from the environment that the job cannot explain, because the job records nothing about the runner around the failure.
- **Guard:** The integration jobs' sampler also records the runner's pressure counters (`/proc/pressure` for CPU, memory and I/O) and free memory every 30 s, and a failed job prints the NATS log. A failed stream delete names its stream and the request limit (`test-sync.test.ts`). The 5 s limit stays: the next stall shows its cause before anyone changes a budget.
