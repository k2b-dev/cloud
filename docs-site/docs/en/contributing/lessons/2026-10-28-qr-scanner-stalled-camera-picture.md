### The QR scanner stayed black in an installed web app on iPad

- **What happened:** In an installed App-PWA on iPadOS, `QrScanner` (`packages/ui/src/inputs/QrScanner.tsx`) showed a black preview while the camera indicator was on, and the scan frame sat in the lower-left corner of the preview (#1994).
- **Root cause:** The scanner attached the camera stream and let qr-scanner 1.4.2 call `video.play()`. WebKit can leave a camera stream unplayed, with a `play()` that never settles or is refused, and the scanner waited for it in silence. qr-scanner also computes its scan region only on `loadedmetadata` and `play`, while a window resize moves the frame. When the picture turns with the device, its size changes without either event, so the stale region put the frame off-centre and cut the area it decodes.
- **Error class:** A component waits for a media element to play without a bound or a visible failure, and it places its overlay from the intrinsic size of the picture only at its first layout.
- **Guard:** `QrScanner` now plays the stream itself before the engine starts. When `play()` is refused or the picture does not start within three seconds, the scanner shows a "Try again" button that attaches the stream again and plays it from the click. The scanner recomputes the scan region on the video's `resize` event and when the preview changes size. `packages/ui/src/inputs/qr-scanner.browser.test.ts` covers a refused and a hanging first `play()`, and a camera that turns from landscape to portrait while the frame must stay centred.
