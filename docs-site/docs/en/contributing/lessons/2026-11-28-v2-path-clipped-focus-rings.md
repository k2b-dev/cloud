### The v2 path cut the focus rings of its places and its "…"

- **What happened:** In a v2 `Path`, a focused place or "…" showed its focus ring only in part: the ring was cut at the path's edges, above and below the "…" and left of the first place. DP2 found it with a W1b test in #4223 and turned off only that ring check.
- **Root cause:** `.k2-path` clips with `overflow: hidden`, so that what does not fit never runs into the bar. A focus ring paints outside its element (the "…" draws a 3 px shadow; a place draws the browser's outline), and the path's clip edge is its padding box, as tall as its line.
- **Error class:** A box that clips its content for the layout also clips what its children paint outside themselves, such as focus rings and shadows, at its edges.
- **Guard:** `.k2-path` cuts with `clip-path: inset(-3px)` instead of an overflow clip. Its content stays cut where it does not fit, and the rings paint whole. A clip path is no scroll box, so nothing can scroll the path, and it works in WebKit, which has no `overflow-clip-margin`. `packages/ui/src/v2/path-focus-ring.browser.test.ts` walks every path of the path demo with the keyboard at 390, 1024 and 1440 px in Chromium and WebKit. For each focused place and "…", it reads how far its ring reaches out and checks that no box up to the page cuts it, by its overflow or by its clip path. `path.browser.test.ts` keeps the path's measured widths.
