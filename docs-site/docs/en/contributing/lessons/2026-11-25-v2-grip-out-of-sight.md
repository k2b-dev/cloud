### The grip of the Kundenakte's info column stood below the window at 1024 px

- **What happened:** On the v2 demo screen "Kundenakte" at 1024 px, the line between the main column and the info column ran below the window, and its grip stood in the middle of the whole line at y = 943 of 900, out of sight. At the same width, the record's phone number in the head ran under the search, star and "Neu" buttons, and the newest entries of the 400 px main column broke their text after every word.
- **Root cause:** The grip was placed by CSS at 50 % of its line; the mockup's script places it in the middle of the part of the line in sight. The start of a head bar could not get narrower than its parts, so a record's identifiers overflowed into the bar's end. The narrow form of a digest entry followed the window (the phone), not its column.
- **Error class:** A layout that is measured only at the mockup's two widths (1440 and 390 px) and in markup without its script misses what moves or overflows at the widths in between.
- **Guard:** `packages/ui/src/v2/grip.browser.test.ts` checks the grip in the middle of the part of its line in sight, before and after scrolling. `docs-site/src/ui/v2/screens.browser.test.ts` checks the record at 1024 px: the identity in the head ends before the actions, and the digest's text keeps whole lines.
