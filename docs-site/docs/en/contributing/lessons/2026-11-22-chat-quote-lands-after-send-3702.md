### A Chat message sent right after Quote left its quote behind in the field

- **What happened:** After Quote, typing and Enter, the message sometimes went out without its quote, and the quote then appeared in the emptied composer. The next Quote showed two quotes side by side: the sent one from Lea Muster and the new one from Ben Schulz. `ChatQuotes.browser.test.ts` "quotes in the dark theme" failed three times in CI with a strict mode violation on `.k2b-chat-composer__quote` (#3702).
- **Root cause:** `ChatConversation` moves the focus to the field as soon as Quote is pressed, but `ChatWorkspace` adds the quote only after `importOnDemand(() => import("./chat-quote-selection"))` resolves, even when the module is already loaded. A send in between read an empty quote list, cleared it, and the quote landed after the send.
- **Error class:** An action applies its state after an asynchronous load, while the interface already accepts the next action that reads this state. The later action runs on the state from before the first one.
- **Guard:** `ChatQuotes.browser.test.ts` "a send right after Quote carries the quote while its code still loads" holds the quote module's chunk, quotes from the message menu, sends and only then releases the chunk. It checks that the posted message carries the quote and that the field holds none. A send now waits for quotes on their way before it reads the draft's quotes.
