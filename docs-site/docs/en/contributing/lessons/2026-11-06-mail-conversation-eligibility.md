### Automatic Mail links used different conversation criteria

- **What happened:** Automatic grouping did not consistently distinguish stored conversations from incomplete threads being synchronized.
- **Root cause:** Candidate lookups decided membership independently, while folder order could leave the original absent.
- **Error class:** Heuristic candidate selection doubles as the final eligibility decision.
- **Guard:** `conversation-eligibility.integration.test.ts` checks one eligibility decision after direct, reverse, shared-reference, subject, and provider selection. It covers recorded From/To/Cc/Reply-To participants, Bcc recipients of own sent mail, mailing lists with matching external List-Post addresses and preserved work state, ticket identifiers, newest-first contact forms with direct own-reply and subject anchors, a ten-conversation candidate bound, unchanged historical subject normalization, alternate quoted candidates, own sent replies, aliases, changed subjects, localized prefixes, missing originals, repeated sync, and unchanged work state. List-only link provenance survives repeat sync and does not establish ordinary sender participation; hydration leaves its work state unchanged. Frozen workflow trigger preconditions retain list-only provenance; status and assignment steps skip with an operator-visible reason while tagging continues. Realistic list headers and workflow/hydration interactions have integration guards. It also checks sender identity before matching a synchronized sent copy. `message-identity.integration.test.ts` preserves different reply authors across folder and out-of-order sync. Existing manual conversation and maintenance suites cover merge and rebuild behavior.
