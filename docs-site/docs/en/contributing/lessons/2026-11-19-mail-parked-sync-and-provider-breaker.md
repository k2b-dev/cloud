### Parked folder syncs and the provider breaker blocked a mailbox's sync

- **What happened:** After #3740 and #3743 landed together, the merge queue failed in "mailboxes of a provider host that stops answering hold back no other mailbox's sync". The test timed out after 90 seconds. Its log showed two mailboxes trying the dead host at once, and one mailbox trying it again right after its breaker had reopened.
- **Root cause:** Two gaps in parking (#3743) next to the breaker (#3740). First, a folder sync that found the lease held parked even while its mailbox could probe the provider, and then tried the lease again with the breaker state it saw before it parked. That try freed this process's single probe for another mailbox, and it could take the lease after the breaker had opened again. Second, folder syncs that parked before the breaker opened waited for a release, but a provider that answered again only closed the breaker and woke nobody. A sync queued by IMAP push then parked behind them instead of running at once. In production the minute scheduler would have woken the line; the test has no scheduler, so it waited until its timeout.
- **Error class:** A wake-up mechanism that covers only some of the events that end a wait. Here the wait ended with a breaker that closed or a probe that finished, not with a lease release.
- **Guard:** "a mailbox that may probe its provider parks no waiter, and a provider that answers again wakes the waiter parked before" in `provider-lease-fairness.integration.test.ts` fails on the old code at both gaps. The dead-host test now ends each breaker pause in Valkey instead of sleeping 19 seconds. Its test worker runs a woken job on any worker, as the job queue does, also when an earlier worker parked it. The assertions are unchanged: no other mailbox is held back, one probe per process, and a push that reaches the host again syncs at once.
