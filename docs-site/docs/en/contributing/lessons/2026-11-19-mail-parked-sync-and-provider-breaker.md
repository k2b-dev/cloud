### A parked folder sync waited for a wake that the dead-host test never brought

- **What happened:** After #3740 and #3743 landed together, the merge queue failed in "mailboxes of a provider host that stops answering hold back no other mailbox's sync". The test timed out after 90 seconds.
- **Root cause:** In the test's recovery phase, the Inbox sync of a recovered mailbox found entries ahead of it in the lease line. They belonged to the jobs of a test worker that had stopped. With #3743 the Inbox sync parked behind them. No job held the lease, so no release woke it. In production the minute scheduler wakes such a line within a minute; the test has no scheduler. Review of the same path found two gaps in the product code. A folder sync parked even while its mailbox could probe the provider, and after parking it tried the lease again with the breaker state it had seen before. That second try could free this process's single probe for another mailbox. Also, folder syncs that parked before a breaker opened were not woken when the provider answered again, so a sync queued by IMAP push waited for the scheduler instead of running at once.
- **Error class:** A wake-up mechanism that covers only some of the events that end a wait: a stopped job, a probe that finished, or a breaker that closed, not only a lease release.
- **Guard:** "a mailbox that may probe its provider parks no waiter, and a provider that answers again wakes the waiter parked before" in `provider-lease-fairness.integration.test.ts` fails on the old code. Waiters no longer park while their mailbox may probe or remembers a breaker window, and `recordProviderReachable` wakes the parked waiter next in line. The dead-host test ends each breaker pause in Valkey instead of sleeping for 19 seconds. Its test worker runs a woken job on any worker, as the job queue does, and the jobs of a stopped worker leave their lines. The test now takes about 20 seconds, and its assertions are unchanged: no other mailbox is held back, one probe per process, and a push that reaches the host again syncs at once.
