### The Spaces overview checked grants per row and scanned every item

- **What happened:** With about 62,000 items in 500 Spaces, the overview counts, the today and upcoming lists, and the activity list read every item and every activity row of the installation. The counts also feed the sidebar's "My work" number on every Spaces page. The overview aggregate took 87 ms with a sequential scan and JIT, where 5 ms is enough for the ten Spaces that the reader can open.
- **Root cause:** Each statement checked access per row with `EXISTS (… space_access … auth.access … principal)`. The principal predicate contains a recursive group subquery that the planner cannot estimate: it expected 261 reachable Spaces instead of 10 and 32,000 items instead of 1,240, so it chose a sequential scan over the space_id indexes.
- **Error class:** A permission check written as a correlated predicate on a hot cross-resource read. The planner must estimate the selectivity of the check per row, and a recursive grant predicate makes it guess.
- **Guard:** The cross-Space reads first resolve the reachable Space ids in one statement (`listReachableSpaceIds`, or the sidebar's Space list), and then filter with `space_id = ANY(ids)`. `packages/spaces/src/service/overview-reach.integration.test.ts` pins the statements of every overview path with `scripts/fixtures/sql-meter.ts` and checks direct and nested group grants, private and `none` Spaces, revocation, and a person without Spaces.
