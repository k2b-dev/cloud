### v2 rows did not show they were selected, and an open detail on the phone had two ways back

- **What happened:** The design QA of Mail on `@k2b/ui/v2` found two gaps. In a selection ("10 ausgewählt") no row showed that it was chosen; only the open row was tinted. On the phone an open message showed its way back in the phone bar and, in its own head, the × that closes it beside the list, because Mail kept that × (`keep`).
- **Root cause:** `Row` had one state for both meanings: `selected` meant "the open one", so there was no way to say "chosen". `Table` already told them apart (`current` and `select`). The phone rule that hides head actions skips kept buttons, and no rule knew that the × and the way back lead to the same place.
- **Error class:** A primitive names a state differently from its siblings, so the second meaning has no place; and two controls with the same destination stay visible because each follows its own rule.
- **Guard:** `Row` now takes `current` (open, as `Nav`, `Tabs` and `Table` name it) and `select: { selected, onChange }` (a box in the lead, the blue tint); the old `selected` is gone, so a caller fails the type check instead of silently meaning the other state. `packages/ui/src/v2/row-select.browser.test.ts` renders a list in a selection on the server, hydrates it, and checks the box's name, tints in light and dark, keyboard and target size in Chromium and WebKit. `packages/ui/src/v2/primitives.browser.test.ts` checks at 390, 767, 768 and 1440 px that an open detail shows either its way back or its ×, never both.
