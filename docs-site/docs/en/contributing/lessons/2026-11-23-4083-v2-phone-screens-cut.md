### The v2 Mail screen cut its chips on the phone while its screen test was green

- **What happened:** On the Fibel page of the v2 Mail screen at 390 px, the line under an open message cut the chip "Angebot" and the text "Ada Renz liest mit". The sender's name broke beside its address, the composer's head broke inside its parts, and an empty strip of 52 px stood above every screen. The screen test passed.
- **Root cause:** An `Inline` is one line with `overflow: hidden` and had no narrow form, so in a narrow pane it cut what had no room. The note after a row's title shrank together with the title, so the title broke before the note was cut. The Fibel frame kept the place of the platform's phone bar but drew nothing in it. The screen test rendered the screens in a frame of its own and compared only the boxes of chosen parts, so it saw neither cut things nor the page's frame.
- **Error class:** A screen test that compares only the boxes of chosen parts, in a frame of its own, misses what a person sees on the page: things cut by a box that clips, text that breaks inside a part, and gaps in the frame.
- **Guard:** `docs-site/src/ui/v2/screens.browser.test.ts` renders each screen on its real Fibel page (`V2ScreenPage`) and checks at 390 px that nothing in the open message is cut, the composer's parts and the sender stay on one line, and the platform's bar stands above the app.
