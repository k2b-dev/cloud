### Coalesced Mail hydration lost requests and repeatedly yielded its place

- **What happened:** New messages could remain without content until a later scheduled pass, and later bodies could finish first.
- **Root cause:** Coalescing merged a submission into a claimed job even after its final pending-body query. Completion released the key without preserving that request. First-attempt hydration also had a 60-second priority disadvantage against envelope syncs and registered a new place after each yielded batch. Newest-first selection explains the observed completion order; it does not establish which delay affected each production message.
- **Error class:** Lost wake-ups at coalesced job completion and repeated priority inversion between stages of one user-visible operation.
- **Guard:** `hydration-scheduling.integration.test.ts` holds a claimed generation after its last body check, imports another message through folder sync, and requires completion without the minute scheduler, after both successful and failed batches. It also exercises four occupied workers, merged requests, persisted publication recovery, and operator timings. `hydration-work.integration.test.ts` covers generation handoff, stale redelivery, transaction rollback, independent publication failure, and expired-delivery fencing. `provider-lease-fairness.integration.test.ts` gives hydration a one-second earlier place and proves that a later sync cannot use its former 60-second head start, while hydration still yields after one source. A later command ends a first-attempt batch and precedes its successor generation. A lost job-heartbeat test requires the source transfer to abort before another body is claimed.
