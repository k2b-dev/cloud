### On the phone, a v2 page of stacked panes did not scroll

- **What happened:** On a phone, a `Pane.Split` with a side column and no open detail (the upload recipe, a record with its info column) stacked its panes into one page, but the page did not scroll. The end of the page, such as the facts of the side column, was cut off under the window, and the floating main action covered its last visible line.
- **Root cause:** The phone rules set the split and its panes to `overflow: visible`, so that the panes form one page. The workspace's main column keeps `overflow: hidden` at every width, so nothing in the chain could scroll. The floating main action also had no room kept after the last line.
- **Error class:** A layout that lets content grow on one level and clips it on the level above leaves content that a person cannot reach. Pictures of the first screen do not show this.
- **Guard:** On the phone, the split of a page without an open detail now scrolls. A workspace with a main action keeps 72 px after the last line of what scrolls. `packages/ui/src/v2/skill-recipes.browser.test.ts` scrolls every recipe and state at 390 px to its end and checks that its last line stands in the window and above the floating main action. It scrolls only boxes with `overflow-y: auto` or `scroll`, because a script can also scroll a box that clips, but a person cannot. The list's held place and the page's scroll key follow the split, and a drawing that fills its pane stays one window high beside an info column (`list.browser.test.ts`, `head-media.browser.test.ts`).
