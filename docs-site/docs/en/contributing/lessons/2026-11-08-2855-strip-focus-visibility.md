### The image strip must keep the open image and the add tile in view

- **What happened:** In a long image strip the open image could sit under the edge fade or scroll out of view. After End, the add tile ended 0.39 px past the strip's edge in desktop WebKit.
- **Root cause:** The strip centered the open image before it had a width, and nothing kept it visible after manual scrolling. Keyboard moves used smooth scrolling. WebKit rounded the fractional viewport width up in `clientWidth`, leaving the calculated scroll range too short.
- **Error class:** Scroll position computed from unsettled or rounded geometry.
- **Guard:** ImageEditor browser guards check the first, middle and last active images outside both fades after opening, late thumbnails, manual scrolling and item rebuilds, including a strip laid out late. Focus guards use extra scroll padding with browser focus scrolling disabled; the Tools host guard requires End to focus Add wholly inside the viewport in Chromium and WebKit. The active item stays sticky outside fades; focus moves use the actual viewport width, round toward visibility and scroll immediately to the browser-clamped ends, with a trailing pixel of padding for rounding.
