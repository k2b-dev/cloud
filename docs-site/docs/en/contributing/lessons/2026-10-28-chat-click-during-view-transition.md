### A Chat test clicked while WebKit still ran the view transition of its last link

- **What happened:** In WebKit, `Chat info reopening > info stays reachable after X and preserves Back on desktop` and `Chat threads on a desktop > the open chat lists its threads with counts; a thread opens in place, by its address, and Back returns` failed on unrelated pull requests and in the merge queue (#2028, #2035, #2020's merge group). A click after a close or a Back succeeded, but the next `waitForURL` timed out after 5 s.
- **Root cause:** Every enhanced link starts a document view transition. In WebKit, the transition of a Chat link stayed active for 3 to 5 s, also after its own animations had finished. During that time, a click went to `<html>` instead of the link, and page replies to Playwright arrived only after the transition ended. The tests waited for the address and the rendered state, which both change before the transition ends. Suspected: on the faster CI runner, the next click or Back of these tests came while the transition was active. The lost click itself was not reproduced locally.
- **Error class:** A test acts on the page while a document view transition is active, so the browser sends the input to `<html>`.
- **Guard:** `viewTransitionEnded(page)` in `packages/ui/test/browser.ts` waits until no view transition is active; `packages/ui/test/browser.browser.test.ts` checks it with a transition that `settled()` leaves active. Both Chat tests await it after each navigation before the next click or Back, and the testing guide asks every test to do so.
