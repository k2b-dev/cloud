### Many places in a v2 sidebar pushed its foot out of sight

- **What happened:** A Grids base with many tables at 1440×900 cut off the sidebar's foot ("Einstellungen") at the bottom of the window (W4).
- **Root cause:** In the v2 `Nav`, only a `list` and a `tree` scroll on their own. Ordinary `groups` stood directly in `.k2-nav`, which clips (`overflow: hidden`) and does not scroll. The foot sits after them with `margin-top: auto`, so many entries pushed it below the sidebar.
- **Error class:** A primitive gives one way of filling a region a scroll area and leaves another way without one, so the same content fits or overflows depending on how the app passes it.
- **Guard:** The groups now stand in `.k2-nav-groups`, a scroll area between the head and the foot without a scroll bar of its own; a tree inside still gives way first and scrolls itself. `packages/ui/src/v2/nav-groups-scroll.browser.test.ts` renders eight groups of eight entries at 1440×900 and 1024×768 in Chromium and WebKit with Cloud's scroll bar of 0.5rem. The title and the foot stay in sight, the groups scroll to the last entry while the head and the foot stay put, and no scroll bar narrows the rows. Above a list of their own (Chat's filters above the conversations) the groups keep their height and only the list gives way; `nav-list.browser.test.ts` checks that no filter is cut, which two areas that both shrink by their share would do.
