### A v2 nav list's own scroll bar cut its rows' lines short in WebKit

- **What happened:** In WebKit, the rows of a `Nav` `list` (#4443), such as Chat's conversations in the sidebar, ended 8 px before the right edge of the sidebar. Their bottom lines stopped short, and `measureV2Layout` reported each line ("stops 0 px short of the left and 8 px short of the right edge of its column"). The Chat v2 follow-up, the first app that puts its rows into the list, had eight red WebKit tests; no test on main was red, because no app on main uses the list yet and the list's own test page had no scroll bar style.
- **Root cause:** `.k2-nav-list` scrolls on its own (`overflow: hidden auto`), but unlike a pane's column it did not hide its scroll bar. Inside Cloud, the shared rule in `index.css` gives every scroller a fixed 0.5rem bar in WebKit and Chromium, which takes room from the content; headless Chromium hides scroll bars, so only WebKit showed it.
- **Error class:** A v2 surface that scrolls on its own without `scrollbar-width: none` and `::-webkit-scrollbar { display: none }`, tested only on a page without Cloud's scroll bar.
- **Guard:** The list hides its bar like a pane's column. `nav-list.browser.test.ts` carries Cloud's 0.5rem scroll bar on its page and asserts that a row, not only the list, reaches the sidebar's edge at 1440 and 1024 px and the phone's at 390 px; it was red in WebKit before the fix (8 px).
