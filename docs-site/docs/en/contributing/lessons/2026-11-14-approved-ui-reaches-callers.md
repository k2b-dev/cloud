### An approved toast and sheet design shipped in the library but not in the apps

- **What happened:** The toast and drawer prototype v2 was approved as a whole, and its three pull requests (#2979, #2981, #2982) built it in `@k2b/ui`. A week later the product still did not look like the prototype. No application sheet passed `detents`, 16 of 17 Undo actions kept the framed button, three loading toasts were replaced by a second toast instead of `update()`, and Cloud's phone panel still dragged by `height` through `attachSheetHandle`, which the plan had included and the sheet pull request left unchanged.
- **Root cause:** The pull requests scoped "done" as the library contract with unchanged callers. The opt-in parts of the design (detents, the ghost Undo, `update()`) need callers to change, and the second sheet mechanism was left for later without a tracked item.
- **Error class:** A design approval delivered as a library capability without a parity check of the callers and sibling mechanisms that make it visible.
- **Guard:** A parity table per approved prototype (detail, built, evidence at `file:line`, screenshot of how apps call it); `sheet-handle.browser.test.ts` and `MoveDialog.browser.test.ts` pin transform-only motion for every sheet with a handle, including an owner that closes it mid-glide, and the Files folder picker as a sheet with detents on phones; `ToastHandle.update()` returns false for a closed toast, so a caller that turns a loading toast into its result can still show a failure.
