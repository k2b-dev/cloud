### A browser launch inside a hook's 5 s default failed nameless on a stalled runner

- **What happened:** On the gate of #3862, `packages/cloud/src/ai/live-dictation-browser.browser.test.ts` failed in `unit (9)` with "a beforeEach/afterEach hook timed out" at 5025 ms. Just before, the launcher printed "chromium launch still pending after 4000 ms; no earlier browser". In CI from 6 to 20 November 2026, a launch took at most 0.4 s in Chromium and 1.2 s in WebKit for files whose only setup is the launch, and the slowest of about 25,000 launches took 4.9 s.
- **Root cause:** The hook, not the launch, failed: the file's `beforeAll` launched the browser with Bun's default 5 s hook timeout. That 5 s is the close budget of the shared launcher (#1473): 2 s graceful close, 2 s kill, 1 s for the rest. A launch had no bound of its own apart from Playwright's 180 s. So a launch that a stalled runner held for 5 s used up the whole hook, and Bun killed the browser without an error that named the launch. 31 hooks kept this default, among them the launcher's own self-test; the other 414 hooks that launch already passed 30 s or more. After a launch timeout, Playwright 1.65 can also leave the failed browser running for 30 s beside the next launch.
- **Error class:** A step with an open-ended duration runs inside a budget that was sized for a different step, so a slow runner turns it into a nameless timeout of the surrounding hook.
- **Guard:** `launchBrowser()` passes its own launch budget (`launchBudgetMs`, 20 s, four times the slowest measured launch) to Playwright, the error names the launch and its budget, and a failed local launch kills its own process group before it reports; `packages/ui/test/browser.browser.test.ts` covers a hung launch. The `browser-launch` check fails a hook in a test file or browser suite that calls `launchBrowser()` lexically inside it with a timeout below `launchHookMs` (30 s) or with a timeout it cannot read; `scripts/checks/browser-launch.test.ts` covers it. Launches inside a test body are pending in #3868.
