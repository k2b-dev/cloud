### A bulk fixture's cleanup used most of its test's budget, and a timeout let it run under the body

- **What happened:** The Chat integration test "pages direct members and rejects adding participant 5,001" failed on a PR that does not touch Chat (#4238, run 46134720695). Its last assertion found no conversation where one was expected, reported as "Unhandled error between tests" after "this test timed out after 30000ms" (#4589). A refused create had not left a row; the expected row was gone.
- **Root cause:** The test creates 4,897 people to fill the 5,000-member cap. The file's `afterEach` deletes them, and every table that names a person checks its key: about 17 s on the test VM, so the test used 24 of its 30 s there. Bun counts `afterEach` into the test's timeout, and once the timeout fires it starts the cleanup while the test body still runs. On a slower CI runner the body was still reading when the cleanup deleted its conversation.
- **Error class:** A bulk fixture whose deletion is a large share of the per-test budget, cleaned in `afterEach`; one slow run then turns into a misleading assertion about data the cleanup already removed.
- **Guard:** The test stands in a `describe` block of its own that deletes those people in its own `afterAll`, right after the test and before the next one, with a hook budget of its own. On the test VM the test now takes about 10 s, and the whole file passes 246/0. Convention: give a fixture of thousands of people the cleanup of its own block, not the shared `afterEach`.
