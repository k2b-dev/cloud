### Crowded calendar bands cut their text and never said what they hid

- **What happened:** On a day with many overlapping entries, the week and day views split the column into lanes so narrow that each word ended in "…" and lines were cut through the middle of their letters. There was no "+n" for the entries that did not fit, so a reader could not tell how many there were.
- **Root cause:** Every overlap got its own lane with no upper limit. The title's clamp assumed a line height that the browser did not use, so the box ended inside a line.
- **Error class:** A layout that grows with its data without a limit, and a clamp computed from a line height that the CSS does not set.
- **Guard:** `packages/ui/src/content/calendar-crowded.browser.test.ts` puts 30 overlapping entries on one day in the week and day views at 1440 and 390 px. It requires whole lines inside each band, a title and time that end above any band laid over them in a phone's week, at most two bands at any time, no "+n" over another "+n" or a band, and that the shown bands plus each "+n" count add up to all entries. It also keeps an open "+n" list open while the clock moves the free time, and checks a right-to-left page. The `capBands` unit tests in `calendar-days.test.ts` pin which entries stay, which go into one "+n", and which bands leave a "+n" room.
