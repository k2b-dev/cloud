### The chat info link decided between open and close only when its transition called back

- **What happened:** In `Chat info reopening` (#2116), a click on Info after closing it changed nothing in WebKit CI. The diagnosis showed no view transition, an idle main thread, and an unchanged address. The cause is not confirmed. A review of the info path found two ways in which a click can do nothing.
- **Root cause:** The link's `onNavigate` chose between `openInfo` and `closeInfo` by reading `panel()`, and an enhanced link runs `onNavigate` inside the view transition's update callback. If the panel changed between the click and the callback, an Open click acted as Close. In addition, `openInfo` and `closeInfo` returned without effect while a chat was still opening (`pendingOpen`).
- **Error class:** A toggle decides from state at callback time instead of from the target the person clicked, and an intent that cannot apply yet is dropped without a trace.
- **Guard:** The link acts on the clicked address (`?panel=info` opens, anything else closes). An info toggle chosen during an open of the same chat runs once that open ends; another chat or a closed chat discards it. Two behavior tests in `ChatWorkspace.behavior.test.tsx` failed before the change: one switches the panel between the click and a held-back transition callback, and one clicks Info during a pending open. In `@k2b/ui` and `@k2b/ssr`, links pass `onNavigate` through unchanged, so no other toggle link decides this way.
