### A reserved scroll gutter cut every line 8 px short of its column edge

- **What happened:** In the flat build, hairlines under table rows, selected rows and detail sections ended 7 to 16 px before the column edge (Pulse on `main`: 28 lines and 4 fills). An alert cell in the detail column stood as a tinted box 19 px inside its column. The maintainer pinned both as "the border does not reach the edge".
- **Root cause:** `AppWorkspace.Main`, `MainPane` and `DetailPanel.Body` reserved `scrollbar-gutter: stable`, and the body also drew an always-present 0.5 rem track. A scroll container clips its content at the gutter, so no child can paint there. Separately, the detail body gave every child that is not a topic the 20 px text-edge margin, also a wrapper that held only an alert cell.
- **Error class:** A protection against one rule (content must not shift when a scrollbar appears) silently broke another (lines run from edge to edge), because no check measured the second rule.
- **Guard:** The audit probe (`flachProbe`) reports 0 short lines and 0 short fills on Pulse's source, error and health views, the same as on the mockup. `detail-panel.render.test.tsx` and `advanced-layout.render.test.tsx` now require that Main and the detail body reserve no gutter and that alert cells, and wrappers holding only alert cells, take no text-edge margin. The guard lane adds the general `edge` category to `layoutProblems`.
