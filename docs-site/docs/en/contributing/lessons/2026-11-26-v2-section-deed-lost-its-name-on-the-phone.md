### A section's deed lost its name on the phone

- **What happened:** On the phone, the deeds in a v2 `Section` head (Notebooks, W2) showed only their symbol, and assistive technology announced them as unnamed buttons. A deed without a symbol showed nothing at all. In the same pass, tabs in a pane's `status` slot (Core admin, S2) were found standing on the pane's edge instead of the text edge.
- **Root cause:** The phone rule that keeps only the symbol hid the button's words with `display: none`. The words were the button's only name, and the rule applied to every deed, with a symbol or without one. The text edge of tabs was set only for the `head` slot, so tabs in the `status` slot had no inset.
- **Error class:** Hiding text with `display: none` also takes it out of the accessibility tree, so a control whose name is its visible words loses that name. An edge rule written for one slot leaves the same primitive without its edge in the next slot.
- **Guard:** The rule now hides the words visually only (clipped to 1 px), and only on a deed whose symbol comes before its words. A deed that leads on (`forward`, › after its words) keeps them. A finger hits 44 px of a symbol-only deed. `describe` skips only words clipped this way (`inset(50%)`, at most 1 px) and still reports a label a person sees cut. `packages/ui/src/v2/section-actions.browser.test.ts` checks this at 390 and 1440 px, in Chromium and WebKit: every deed is found by its name, the words stay in sight where they should, the outline and `measureV2Layout` report no problems, a narrowed deed is still reported as cut, and tabs in a detail's `status` slot start on the text edge.
