### Floating pills had more space above and below their buttons than at the sides

- **What happened:** The Notebooks editor pills were 48 px high around 24 px buttons: 12 px above and below each round button background, but 7 px at the sides. The `PdfViewer` bar was a second pill with its own sizes, 54 px high around 44 px buttons.
- **Root cause:** Each pill set a fixed height and its own padding (`height: 3rem` and `padding: 0.25rem 0.375rem` in Notebooks, `padding: 0.25rem` in `content-parity.css`). The height did not follow from the control size, so the vertical space grew as the controls got smaller. The red pill that replaces the Notebooks pills copied the height through a third value (`--k2b-toolbar-control: 2.375rem`).
- **Error class:** A box of controls gets a fixed height instead of control size plus one inset, so the space around the controls differs between axes and between pills of the same kind.
- **Guard:** `--k2b-pill-control` and `--k2b-pill-inset` in `@k2b/ui` set every pill of small controls, and the floating `Toolbar` uses `--k2b-toolbar-inset` on all sides. Browser tests measure the space between each pill's edge and its buttons above, below, and at the sides at desktop and phone width: `packages/notebooks/src/frontend/[id]/_components/editor/EditorToolbar.browser.test.ts` (both pills and the red pill) and `packages/ui/src/content/pdf-viewer.browser.test.ts`.
