### A long record name took three rows of the head on the phone

- **What happened:** On the phone, Weather showed a place with a long name in a `RecordHead`. The avatar, the name and the "…" of the head each took a row of their own, and the head was about 140 px high. A first fix let the kinds wrap into a second row. With four kept symbols in the Kundenakte head, the name then shrank to 40 px beside its kinds, and the head grew to 85 px instead of the mockup's 57 px.
- **Root cause:** The phone rules let the head bar and its start wrap (`flex-wrap: wrap`) without an order. The name kept its full width and pushed the avatar and the actions to other rows. After the first fix, the kinds kept their width and the name gave way instead.
- **Error class:** A wrap rule without an order. Wrapping decides what gives way, so it has to name what comes first: here the name before the kinds.
- **Guard:** On the phone the head is one row of 57 px. The name keeps at least 8ch of its own type and is cut with "…". A kind without room beside it wraps below the row, where the start cuts it off. `packages/ui/src/v2/record-head.browser.test.ts` renders a very long name with two kinds and four symbols at 390 px. It checks the 57 px height, the name's 8ch, that the avatar, the actions and every visible kind stand on the name's row, and that no kind is half cut. A second test checks that 1440 px keeps the 56 px head with both kinds. The demo screens keep the mockup's 57 px head.
