### An open hint over the next sidebar row delayed that row's hover in WebKit

- **What happened:** In WebKit, `sidebar-row-label-room.browser.test.ts` › "right-to-left: a long label fades out right before the count or the revealed hover action, at rest, hovered and active" failed at random with `swap hovered with a wider group: label reaches 134, first control at 178` (#2085). It blocked the 0.51 release gate. The flake started after #2058 gave sidebar rows their name hint.
- **Root cause:** A `Tooltip` surface took the pointer (`pointer-events: auto`) and stays in the top layer for its 120 ms exit. The hint of the row hovered before lay over the next row. When the pointer moved there, WebKit applied `:hover` to that row only 108–200 ms later, after the hint had left; without hints it took 3–8 ms. The test read the row while its hover actions were still hidden and took the screenshot after they showed. Cloud users in Safari saw the same late reveal.
- **Error class:** A non-interactive overlay takes the pointer, so the control under it gets its hover late.
- **Guard:** `.k2b-tooltip` has `pointer-events: none`, as its contract ("non-interactive hint") says. `sidebar-rail-hint.browser.test.ts` checks in Chromium and WebKit that the point under an open hint hits what lies beneath; it fails without the fix. The label test also waits for `:hover` after each move, as the testing guide now asks. Unchanged, it passed 15 of 15 WebKit runs with the fix; before, it failed 3 of 12.
